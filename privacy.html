Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

The short version

  • WNN has no accounts, and we do not collect your personal information.
  • Your Screen Time and app-blocking data never leaves your iPhone. We cannot see it.
  • Calendar access, if you grant it, is read on the device only and is never uploaded.
  • Our subscription provider receives an anonymous identifier and basic device information — nothing that identifies you by name.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not run ads or advertising trackers.
01

Who we are

WNN is an iPhone application operated by Matthew Ari Freedberg, an individual developer based in the United States ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use WNN, and it applies to the app and to this website.

Questions about privacy can be sent to wnnapp1@gmail.com.

02

Information we do not collect

WNN does not require an account and has no sign-up. We do not collect, store, or receive:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Your date of birth, gender, or any demographic information
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Any payment card or bank details
  • Advertising identifiers, and we do not use the App Tracking Transparency prompt because we do not track you

Everything you set up in WNN — your mode, schedules, blocked app selections, streaks, scores, and preferences — is stored locally on your iPhone. It is not sent to us and it is not stored on any server we control.

03

Screen Time and app blocking

WNN blocks apps using Apple's Family Controls, ManagedSettings, and DeviceActivity frameworks, which are the only means Apple provides for one app to restrict another.

Apple designed these frameworks so that the developer cannot see what the user selected. When you choose apps to block, iOS returns opaque tokens rather than app names, and those tokens are only meaningful on your device. As a result:

  • We never learn which apps you block.
  • We never learn how you use your apps — usage times, pickups, and screen time totals are not visible to us.
  • Your selections and activity data stay on the device and are not transmitted anywhere.

Screen Time information is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. Granting WNN Screen Time access does not give us access to that data.

04

Calendar access

WNN can optionally read events from your device calendar so that schedules can be built around what is already booked. This access is entirely optional and the app works without it.

  • Calendar events are read on the device only, at the moment they are needed.
  • We do not upload, copy, store, or transmit your events, invitees, or event details.
  • WNN does not write to, delete, or change your calendar entries unless you explicitly ask it to.
  • You can withdraw calendar access at any time at Settings › WNN › Calendars.
05

Subscriptions and the limited data involved

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never see your payment method, billing address, or Apple Account details. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

To know whether your subscription is active, WNN uses RevenueCat, Inc., a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • A randomly generated, anonymous app user ID that we cannot connect to your identity
  • Your purchase receipt from Apple, which shows the product bought, purchase and expiry dates, and trial or renewal status
  • Basic device and app information such as device model, operating system version, app version, country, and IP address at the time of the request

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, or any of your schedules, blocked apps, or calendar data. It acts as our service provider and processes this information only to verify entitlements on our behalf. Its practices are described in RevenueCat's own privacy policy.

We use this information for one purpose: to determine whether the app should unlock paid features, and to understand aggregate subscription totals such as how many subscriptions are active. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

06

Notifications

WNN can send local notifications and alarms to tell you when a window is starting or ending. These are scheduled by the app on your device. They do not pass through our servers, and we do not receive confirmation of whether you opened them. You can turn notifications off at Settings › WNN › Notifications.

07

Analytics, advertising, and selling data

  • WNN contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • We do not build behavioural profiles and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are used under California law.
  • Apple may provide us with aggregate, anonymised App Store statistics such as download and crash totals. This data does not identify individual users.
08

This website

This website is a set of static pages with no sign-up, no comment fields, and no advertising or analytics trackers. It loads a web font from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers that includes your IP address; that request is subject to Google's own privacy policy. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security and reliability.

09

Children

WNN is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for us to hold. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in some way, contact wnnapp1@gmail.com and we will investigate and delete anything found.

10

Retention and security

Since your schedules and settings stay on your device, retention is in your hands: deleting WNN removes that data from the iPhone. Subscription records held by RevenueCat are kept for as long as needed to manage entitlements, and are governed by RevenueCat's retention practices.

Data on your device is protected by iOS security, including the device passcode and Apple's encryption. Requests between the app and RevenueCat are encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but the amount of data at risk is deliberately kept as close to zero as the app allows.

11

Your rights in California (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected, used, disclosed, or sold
  • Access a copy of the personal information held about you
  • Delete personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights

In practice, we collect no personal information that identifies you, we sell nothing, and we share nothing for advertising. There is therefore no sale to opt out of and no profile to disclose. You may still make a request at wnnapp1@gmail.com and we will respond within the time the law allows, usually 45 days. For the anonymous subscription record, tell us your purchase date and plan so we can locate it.

12

Your rights in the EU and UK (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Legal bases

  • Contract. Processing the anonymous subscription identifier and receipt is necessary to provide the paid features you signed up for.
  • Consent. Screen Time and calendar permissions are granted by you and can be withdrawn at any time in iOS Settings, without affecting anything processed beforehand.
  • Legitimate interests. Keeping the app secure and working correctly, and understanding aggregate subscription totals.

Transfers

We are based in the United States, and RevenueCat processes data in the United States. Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA or UK, it is done under appropriate safeguards, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

To exercise any right, email wnnapp1@gmail.com. Because we hold no information identifying you, we may be unable to locate a record without details such as your purchase date and plan, and in some cases we may not be able to identify you at all.
