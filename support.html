Managing your subscription

Cancel or change your plan

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Select WNN and choose Cancel or change plan

Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Free trial

New subscribers get 7 days free. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. You can use the app for the full trial period either way.

Refunds

All purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple rather than by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple declines and you think the situation warrants another look, email us and we'll do what we can.

Restore a purchase

If you've subscribed before — on this phone or another one — open the paywall in WNN and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you originally purchased with.

Common questions

  • Blocking isn't working

    Check that Screen Time permission is still granted: Settings › Screen Time, and confirm WNN still appears under apps with access. Permission can be revoked by an iOS update, a device restore, or by turning Screen Time off and on.

    If permission looks right and blocking still fails, email us with your iOS version.

  • I can still open a blocked app

    WNN blocks apps on the device it's installed on. It can't block the same app on a different phone, a computer, or a website version accessed through a browser. It also can't block anything during a period you didn't schedule.

  • I'm locked out and need to stop a session

    Every lockdown has an escape hatch. On the lock screen, tap End lockdown early. It's deliberately a little effortful, but it always works — you're never permanently locked out of your own device.

  • Prices won't load on the paywall

    This usually means the device can't reach the App Store. Check your connection, close and reopen the app, and try again. If it persists, email us.

  • My schedule times look wrong

    Schedules build from the times you enter and your device clock. If you've travelled across time zones, reopen the mode and confirm your wake and school or work times are still correct.

  • Notifications aren't arriving

    Check Settings › Notifications › WNN and make sure notifications are allowed. Focus modes and Do Not Disturb can also suppress alarms depending on your settings.

  • Will I lose my data if I delete the app?

    Yes. Your schedules, streak, and history are stored on your device only — nothing is on a server, so deleting the app deletes that data permanently. Your subscription itself is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored.

  • Can I use WNN on more than one device?

    Your subscription follows your Apple ID, so you can install WNN on your other devices and tap Restore Purchases. Blocking is configured separately on each device.
