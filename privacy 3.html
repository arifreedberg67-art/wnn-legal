Privacy Policy

For WNN · Last updated July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WNN ("we," "us," or "the app") handles information when you use the WNN mobile application. WNN is operated by Matthew Ari Freedberg, an individual developer based in the United States.

The short version: WNN is built to keep your information on your own device. We do not require an account, we do not ask for your name or email, and we cannot see what apps you block or how you use the app.

Information We Do Not Collect

WNN does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or mailing address
  • Account credentials — WNN has no accounts and no sign-up
  • Payment or credit card details — all purchases are processed by Apple, and we never receive your payment information
  • Your location
  • Contacts, photos, messages, or files
  • The specific apps you choose to block, your schedules, your streaks, or your usage history

Information Stored on Your Device

WNN stores your settings and activity locally on your iPhone. This includes your selected schedules and routines, which apps or categories you have chosen to restrict, your streak and consistency history, and your app preferences.

This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded to us, and we have no ability to access it. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

Screen Time and Family Controls

WNN uses Apple's Family Controls and Managed Settings frameworks to block distracting apps. Apple designed these frameworks so that your app selections are handled privately on your device and are never exposed to the developer. We cannot see which apps you select, and that information never reaches us or any third party.

Calendar Access

If you choose to import events from your calendar, WNN reads your calendar entries on your device in order to build your schedule around them. This calendar information is used only on your device and is never transmitted to us or stored on any server. You can decline or revoke calendar access at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Notifications

WNN sends notifications and alarms to support your schedules and blocks. These are delivered through Apple's notification system. You can turn notifications off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Subscriptions and Third-Party Services

WNN offers auto-renewing subscriptions. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

To manage subscription status, WNN uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated anonymous identifier along with basic technical information such as your device model, operating system version, country, and the status of your purchase. This is used solely to confirm whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, or payment details. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Apple may also provide us with aggregate, anonymized sales and performance statistics through App Store Connect. These reports do not identify individual users.

Children's Privacy

WNN is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, no such information about a child would be collected in the ordinary course of using the app.

Your Rights and Choices

Because WNN keeps your information on your own device and does not collect personal information, there is generally no personal data held by us to access, correct, or delete.

You remain in control at all times: you can revoke Screen Time, calendar, or notification permissions in your iPhone's Settings, manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID account settings, and delete all app data by deleting the app.

Depending on where you live — including under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), other U.S. state privacy laws, and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to appeal a decision about such a request. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising purposes, and we do not engage in targeted advertising. If you have a request or question about your rights, contact us at the email below and we will respond.

Security

Because your information stays on your device, it is protected by your iPhone's own security, including your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and Apple's device encryption. We hold no database of user information that could be breached.

Changes to This Policy

If WNN adds features that change how information is handled, we will update this policy and revise the date at the top. Significant changes will be reflected here before the relevant feature becomes available.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy can be sent to wnnapp1@gmail.com. We aim to respond within a reasonable period.
