05:00Wake

Most days don't get lost at once.They get lost in pieces.

WNN blocks the apps that take those pieces — on a schedule you set in advance, so the decision is already made by the time the urge shows up.

07:30The work

What it actually does

WNN uses Apple's Screen Time controls to lock distracting apps out of the hours you've committed to. You choose what gets blocked and when. Once a block starts, it holds.

Blocking is only half of it. WNN builds the day around the block — focus periods, breaks, and a rhythm you can keep. A schedule, not just a wall.

15:00The honest part

What WNN won't do

It won't gamify discipline or reward you with confetti. It tracks the things that matter — your integrity score, your streak, and a chart that shows honestly how your consistency is trending.

There's a real escape hatch if you need out. No dark patterns, no guilt screens. But the friction is deliberate.

Blocking depends on your device. WNN relies on Apple's Screen Time frameworks, so a permission change, a restart, or another device can affect a block. It's a support for your discipline, not a replacement for it.
