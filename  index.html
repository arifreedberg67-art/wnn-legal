How it works

Three moments, and only one of them needs willpower.

The night before

Choose your windows

Pick a mode or build your own schedule, then choose which apps and categories go dark. You set it once, while you are calm and clear-headed.

When the window opens

The block starts on its own

WNN uses Apple's Screen Time framework to hide the apps you chose, at the times you chose. Nothing to remember, nothing to switch on.

While it runs

You get the hour back

A lock screen widget shows the window you are in and how long is left. When it ends, your apps come back on their own.

What WNN does not do

No account. No sign-up. Nothing about your day leaves your phone.

No accounts

There is no email to enter, no password to make, and no profile to fill in. Open the app and start.

Screen Time stays local

Apple's Family Controls framework keeps your app selections on the device. WNN never receives them and never uploads them.

No tracking

No advertising identifiers, no analytics profile, no data sold or shared. Read the full privacy policy.

Before you download

WNN needs Screen Time permission to work.

App blocking on iPhone is only possible through Apple's Screen Time and Family Controls system, so WNN asks for that permission the first time you set up a schedule. If the permission is turned off later, or Screen Time is restricted on the device, blocking stops until it is granted again. There is more on this — and what to do when a block does not start — on the support page.
